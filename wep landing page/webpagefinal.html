Who we are

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Govind Shukla, its Founder and CEO, and is based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. It runs as a Section 8 non-profit, meaning it exists purely to further its social mission rather than to generate profit for anyone.

Since then, the foundation has grown into a network of roughly 200 volunteers working across 28 states, running six ongoing causes that have reached more than 50,000 beneficiaries — from meals delivered on the street to saplings planted in fields to internships that turn into careers.

None of this happens through one big programme. It happens through six specific, named projects, each pointed at a different kind of need. You can read about all six below.

The six projects

Each one is run separately, with its own volunteers and its own goals, so that progress in one never comes at the cost of another.

Seva Serving humanity with compassion

Volunteers cook, pack and hand-deliver meals, and collect and distribute clothing to families going without. The project has now crossed 50,000 meals and garments distributed.

Why it matters: no one should be deciding between a meal and rent — Seva removes that choice, one delivery at a time.

Bachpanshala Nurturing young minds, building bright futures

Learning sessions for children from underprivileged backgrounds, covering school subjects, basic digital literacy, and everyday life skills alongside classroom support.

Why it matters: a child's postcode shouldn't decide their education — Bachpanshala keeps that door open.

Jeev Empowering lives, spreading compassion

Animal welfare on the ground: the volunteer network feeds more than 50 stray animals a day, and steps in to rescue and protect injured or at-risk strays.

Why it matters: a humane city looks after every life inside it, not only the human ones.

Udaan Soaring towards a brighter future

Working directly with women's self-help groups in rural areas on skill-building, financial independence, and menstrual hygiene awareness — a topic still under-discussed in many villages.

Why it matters: when a woman earns her own income and understands her own health, the whole household's prospects shift with her.

Prakriti Plant for a better tomorrow

Over 20,000 saplings planted so far, alongside work with farmers on eco-friendly, sustainable agricultural practices.

Why it matters: a tree planted today is shade, clean air and food security that will outlast any one of us.

Vikas Skilling India's next workforce

Structured internships in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing and social work — over 30,000 interns trained in the last four years.

Why it matters: a skill someone can put on a résumé is a door that stays open long after the internship ends.
